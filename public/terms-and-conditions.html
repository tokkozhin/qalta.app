Shared spaces

Shared spaces require an active paid subscription. When you share a space, its data is stored on our servers and synced to every member. Every member can view, add, change, and delete records in that space, and those changes reach everyone. Invite only people you trust, and do not upload data you are not allowed to share.

The owner can remove members, stop sharing, or delete the space. Stopping sharing or deleting the space removes its data from our servers for all members; each member keeps a copy on their own device. A space that has stopped sharing cannot be shared again. If your subscription ends, syncing pauses until you renew. We are not responsible for changes other members make to a shared space.
