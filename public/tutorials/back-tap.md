# Create a Qalta expense with Back Tap

Use Back Tap to launch a Qalta expense shortcut with three taps on the back of your iPhone.

[Add Back tap expense to Shortcuts](https://www.icloud.com/shortcuts/876c51a66dd1491ea01d86fa92e8247a)

## Set up Triple Tap

1.  Open the link above on your iPhone and tap **Add Shortcut** in Shortcuts. It will appear as **Back tap expense**.

2.  Open **Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap**.

3.  Tap **Triple Tap**, scroll to **Shortcuts**, then select **Back tap expense**.

4.  Now triple-tap the back of your iPhone. The shortcut asks for an amount and category, then creates the expense in **Qalta**.

**You need Qalta.** When it runs, the shortcut asks for the amount and expense category before creating an expense. Make sure Qalta is installed.
