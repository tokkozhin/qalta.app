Qalta Support

Get help with the app, AI assistant connections, and your data. Support is available in English and Russian.

Contact us

Email help.qalta.app@outlook.com. Include your Qalta and iOS versions, the assistant you use, when the issue happened, and steps to reproduce it. Screenshots with personal information hidden can help.

Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, access tokens, or full financial statements.

Connect an AI assistant

  1. Sign in to Qalta and check that the cloud space you want to use is syncing. Private spaces stored only on your device are not available to the connector.
  2. If your assistant supports custom MCP connectors, enter https://mcp.qalta.app/mcp as the server URL.
  3. Sign in through Qalta and review the permissions before approving access.
  4. Start with a read request, such as “How much did I spend on groceries this month?”

Troubleshooting

If sign-in fails, disconnect the connector and connect again. If a space is missing, check your account and syncing in Qalta. If a change does not appear, check your records in the app before repeating the request to avoid duplicates.

Disconnect access

Open Connected AI agents, sign in to your Qalta account, and choose Revoke for the agent. Also remove the connection in your assistant’s settings. Disconnecting does not undo changes already saved.

Your account and data

Contact us about account deletion, privacy, or incorrect changes to your data. You can also delete your account in the app. Read our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for details.
