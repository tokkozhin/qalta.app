Bank notification automation

iOS 27 · English

Qalta can save a purchase from a bank app notification. This requires iOS 27.

Steps

  1. Open the Shortcuts app.
  2. Create a new automation and choose Notification.
  3. Pick the bank app.
  4. Add an action: Qalta → Log Bank Expense.
  5. Pass the notification Body into Notification body.
  6. Optionally set Note, Account, and Category, or leave them as Ask Each Time.
